One quiet friend for your whole field

GeeKOc: “I read the twenty sites, so you read one.”

  • Give it a few keywords, a CV, your ORCID or your GitHub, or nothing at all: it learns from what you open
  • It ranks everything on your device. No server, no account, no telemetry
  • It only suggests. You follow, apply and write by hand, always
  • Your phone and your computer share it, through your own Google Drive or a private repo, if you want

  • My month: your rank from Soldier to General, and a card to share

Privacy & trust

What it will never do

Every promise here is a file you can check: myGeeKy is MIT-licensed and open to read. The privacy policy.

Never acts as you

It never follows, stars, posts or comments. There is no code for it.

Never leaves your device

The model runs on your computer or phone. No server, no telemetry.

Never holds your keys

Tokens stay in your system's keyring, checked for least privilege.

Never takes your data

Shared only through your own Google Drive or a private repo, if you want.

Never sends behind your back

If it breaks, you read the whole report first, and you decide.

Get it

Up and running in two minutes

🪟 Windows

  1. Download MyGeeKySetup.exe
  2. Next → Next → Finish

Nothing to install first, no admin rights. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC” (not code-signed yet), click More info → Run anyway.

🐧 Linux · 🍎 macOS · anywhere

curl -fsSL https://mygeeky.org/install.sh | sh

or with pip:

pip install mygeeky
mygeeky setup

Run the one-liner again to update. Macs get myGeeKy.app in Launchpad and Spotlight.

📱 Android

Coming to Google Play soon. Meanwhile, the test version is on every GitHub release. Already use myGeeKy on your computer? One scan brings your profile over.

Then type what you work on, or nothing at all. No ORCID, no GitHub? No account needed: myGeeKy learns your field from what you open and like, and you can link GitHub, ORCID or Google Drive any time later.
