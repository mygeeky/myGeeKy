## Full description (4000 — this draft is about 1,900)

```
myGeeKy is a quiet companion for people who work at a computer: developers and computer
scientists, bioinformaticians and data scientists, and researchers who analyse their own
experiments.

It does three things.

GROW
Pick a skill and follow a reviewed protocol: a short path of steps, each one pointing at
a real tutorial, repository, paper or course that somebody checked by hand. Fourteen of
them so far, from Git and GitHub teamwork to statistics for experiments, from a first web
app to single-cell RNA-seq, from algorithms for interviews to turning images into numbers.

PROVE IT
Every protocol has a quiz. Each question quotes a sentence that is really on one of that
protocol's own sources, so an answer can always be checked against the page it came from.
Ten questions an attempt, a mix of easy, applied and the real pitfalls. Only your best
score counts, one attempt a day, and you can flag a question you think is wrong.

FIND YOUR CHANCES
Jobs, funding calls and conferences, each one read and verified before it is listed, and
ranked by how well it fits what you actually work on. Deadlines are checked, and anything
past its date disappears by itself.

There is also what your week looked like, the papers and repositories worth your attention,
and badges for the things you got done.

HOW IT TREATS YOU

No account to create. You sign in with GitHub or ORCID, and that is the only account there
is — myGeeKy has no server of its own and no profile of you.

Nothing is collected. Your token stays in your phone's secure store. Your scores, your
progress and what you have read stay on the phone. There are no adverts, no analytics and
no tracking.

Pair it with the desktop app by scanning a QR code, and the two show the same things.

myGeeKy is free and open source. The code, the reviewed protocols and every quiz question
are public at github.com/mygeeky/myGeeKy, and anyone can check them, correct them or add
to them.
```
