<a name="see"></a>
## 👀 Helps you see what is moving in your field

> *“I read the twenty sites, so you read one.”*

**Papers** gaining citations fastest and the tools behind them, your field as a **daily market**, **models**
trending on Hugging Face, and **news** from labs, journals and the press: all ranked for *your* keywords, not
for everybody's.

<a name="learn"></a>
## 🌱 Helps you learn what comes next

> *“You haven't used Nextflow yet, and five repos near your work just did. Shall we start?”*

**Any skill becomes a road map.** *R for data analysis* turns into: R basics → statistics with R → a real
project to rerun → your own work. A level check **skips what you already know**, using your own repos as
evidence.

<details>
<summary><b>More about Learn</b></summary>
<br>

The level check shows its evidence (*“6 of your repos use R”*), and you can overrule it.

For a skill nobody has written up, a small model **on your machine** (about 400 KB) searches code and papers
and sorts what it finds into stages. **14 protocols are written and reviewed**, from *your first pull request*
to *statistics for experiments: which test, and why*.

<sub>Software, data, statistics, figures and pipelines, <b>never laboratory or bench protocols</b>:
<a href="https://github.com/mygeeky/myGeeKy/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md#what-mygeeky-does-not-teach">why</a>.</sub>

</details>

<a name="meet"></a>
## 👥 Helps you meet the people working on the same problems

> *“These four work on what you work on, and they tend to follow back.”*

**People near your work**, with how likely each is to follow back, what they **pushed in the last hour**, and
**repos worth your first pull request**. **You follow, star and message by hand, always.** myGeeKy introduces;
it never acts for you.

leaderboard, nothing for anyone else to see.

<a name="catch"></a>
## 🎯 Helps you catch the opportunities while they are still open

> *“Two deadlines this week. Both still open; I checked the page myself.”*

**Jobs, funding, conferences and the scholarships that come back every year.** Each one is **checked against
its own page** before you see it, sorted by **deadline**, and **gone the moment it passes**.

</tr>
</table>

<details>
<summary><b>More about Catch, and 🗓 Seasons</b></summary>
<br>

The board is never a list of things you are already too late for. A listing written in **Arabic, French,
German, Spanish or Chinese** is read, translated and still checked against its own page, then shown to you in
English. Filter by country, because that applies to all of them.

**Seasons is for the ones that come back every year.** A scholarship does not stop being real when it closes:
it returns next April, and *the preparing takes longer than the applying*. So myGeeKy says *“expected in about
12 weeks”* while there is still time to get transcripts certified and a proposal written, and tells you **how
many years of evidence** stand behind that expectation.

It never pretends to know next year's date: a window it read on a page says **open now**, anything ahead says
**expected**.

</details>

## 🎮 Helps you play your field

> *“Real tool or made up? Snakemake, yes. Bioflux, no. And now you know why.”*

**Play with GeeKOc: fourteen short games made from your own field.** Jump the bugs in GeeKOc run, steer names into
the right net, climb a pipeline in Rise, find the way home through doors that ask questions, whack the bugs and
spare the good practices. Every question has a **level, field terms and a source**, a mistake **always says why**,
and what you play teaches your Model. GeeKOc jumps, cheers and sulks along with you.

```bash
pip install mygeeky
mygeeky setup
```

<sub>Run the one-liner again to update. Macs get <b>myGeeKy.app</b> in Launchpad and Spotlight.</sub>

<a name="never"></a>
## 🔒 What it will never do

> *“I keep all of this on your computer. I never post for you, and I never tell anyone you were here.”*

|  |  |  |
|:-:|---|---|
| ✋ | **Never acts as you.** | You look, you decide, you act. |
| 🧠 | **Never leaves your machine.** | No server, no account, no telemetry. |
| 🔑 | **Never holds your keys.** | They stay in your system's keyring. |
| 📦 | **Never takes your data.** | It stays on your computer. |
| 📨 | **Never sends anything behind your back.** | You read every report before it goes. |

<details>
<summary><b>The promises in full</b></summary>
<br>

- ✋ **Never acts as you.** It never follows, stars, forks, posts or comments: **there is no code for it
  anywhere in the project.**
- 🧠 **Never leaves your machine.** The model that ranks all of this runs on your computer, because there is
  no server.
- 🔑 **Never holds your keys.** Tokens live in your operating system's keyring, checked for least privilege,
  **never logged and never printed.**
- 📦 **Never takes your data.** It stays on your devices, plus, if you want them shared, **your own Google Drive or a private repo you own**.
- 📨 **Never sends anything behind your back.** If it breaks, you read the whole report first, with your name,
  email, tokens and folders already stripped out, and **you** decide whether it goes.
