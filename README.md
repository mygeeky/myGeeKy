<h1 align="center">myGeeKy</h1>

<h3 align="center">Your geeky friend who helps you grow, quietly.</h3>

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mygeeky/myGeeKy/main/docs/geekoc/geekoc-grows.svg" width="660" alt="GeekOc, the myGeeKy octopus, watching a plant grow skills, knowledge, collaboration and opportunity, safe under glass">
</p>

<p align="center">
  <b>myGeeKy is a quiet companion that helps you grow.</b><br>
  Tell it what you work on. It brings back what to learn next, what is rising in your field,<br>
  who works on the same problems, and what is open to apply for before it closes —<br>
  <b>and then checks that you really learned it.</b>
</p>

<p align="center">
  It lives on your own computer, in a narrow panel beside your work.<br>
  <b>No feed. No likes. No posting. Nothing to scroll.</b>
</p>

## 🌱 Learn something, and prove it

<table>
<tr>
<td width="20%" align="center" valign="middle">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mygeeky/myGeeKy/main/docs/geekoc/geekoc-grow.png" width="160" alt="GeekOc thinking">
</td>
<td valign="middle">

> *“You haven't used Nextflow yet — and five repos near your work just did. Shall we start?”*

**Any skill becomes a road map.** *R for data analysis* turns into: R basics → statistics with R → a real
project to rerun → your own work. A level check **skips what you already know**, using your own repos as
evidence (*“6 of your repos use R”* — which you can overrule).

For a skill nobody has written up, a small model **on your machine** (about 400 KB) searches code and papers
and sorts what it finds into stages. **14 protocols are written and reviewed**, from *your first pull request*
to *statistics for experiments: which test, and why*.

## 💡 Why it is not like the others

|  |  |
|---|---|
| 🧠 **It thinks on your machine.** | The model that ranks all of this is **on your computer** — not on a server, because there is no server. No account, no sign-up, no telemetry. If you sync between computers or to your phone, it travels through a **private repo you own**. |
| 🎯 **Chances expire.** | Every job, grant and conference is verified against its own page and **removed once the deadline passes**. A board that never forgets is a board you cannot trust. |
| 📚 **The learning is checked.** | Code in a question is **run**, sums are **recomputed**, and every answer shows the **source and the sentence** it came from — before the question is ever shipped to you. |
| ✋ **It never acts for you.** | It never follows, stars, forks, posts or messages anybody. **There is no code for it anywhere in the project.** You look, you decide, you act. |

### 🪟 Windows

1. Download **[MyGeeKySetup.exe](https://github.com/mygeeky/myGeeKy/releases/latest)** (under *Assets*)

pip install mygeeky

## 🔒 What it will never do

> *“I keep all of this on your computer. I never post for you, and I never tell anyone you were here.”*

- **Never follows, stars, forks, posts or comments for you** — no code for it exists in the project.
- **No server, no account, no telemetry.** It talks only to public research APIs and GitHub.
- **Your data stays yours** — on your computer, plus a **private** repo you own if you want sync.
- **Tokens only in your OS keyring**, checked for least privilege, never logged or printed.
- **Nothing is ever sent automatically.** If it breaks, you see the whole report — your name, email, tokens and
  folders already removed — and you decide whether to send it.

<sub><a href="https://github.com/mygeeky/myGeeKy/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md#safety">Safety in full</a> ·
